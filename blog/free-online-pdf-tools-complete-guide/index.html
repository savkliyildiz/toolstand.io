Free Online PDF Tools: Merge, Split, Convert Without Software

PDF tools are among the most searched utilities online — and among the most dangerous. Most "free online PDF tools" require uploading your document to their server, where it could be stored, analyzed, or leaked. ToolStand's PDF tools process everything in your browser.

PDF Manipulation Tools

  • PDF Merge: Combine multiple PDFs into a single document. Drag and drop to reorder pages. Client-side merging using PDF-lib — your documents never leave your device. Perfect for combining scanned chapters, invoices, or reports.
  • PDF Split: Extract specific pages or split a PDF into individual files. Choose page ranges or split by every N pages. Ideal for breaking large PDFs into manageable chunks.

PDF Conversion Tools

  • Image to PDF: Convert JPG, PNG, WebP, and other images to PDF. Merge multiple images into a single multi-page PDF. Control page size and orientation.
  • PDF to Image: Extract each page of a PDF as a separate image. Choose PNG or JPG output format with configurable quality. Perfect for sharing individual pages on social media or in presentations.

Privacy-First PDF Processing

Financial documents, legal contracts, medical records — PDFs often contain your most sensitive information. Server-based PDF tools are a privacy nightmare: your document is transmitted to a third party, stored temporarily (or permanently), and processed on infrastructure you don't control.

ToolStand's PDF tools use browser-native JavaScript libraries (PDF-lib, jsPDF) to process files entirely in memory. Your documents are never uploaded to any server. Close the tab, and every byte of your PDF is gone from memory. For maximum security, disconnect from the internet after loading the tool — it works fully offline.

Performance Considerations

Browser-based PDF processing works best with files under 50MB. Very large PDFs (100MB+) may push browser memory limits. For those cases, desktop tools like PDFsam or command-line tools like Ghostscript are more appropriate. For 95% of everyday PDF tasks — merging a few invoices, splitting a report, converting images to PDF — browser tools are faster and more secure.
