Writing & Content Productivity

  • Word & Character Counter: Count words, characters (with and without spaces), sentences, paragraphs, and estimated reading time. Essential for content creators hitting word count targets or SEO specialists optimizing meta descriptions.

  • Text Diff Checker: Compare two texts side by side. Color-coded LCS diff algorithm. Perfect for reviewing document revisions, code changes, or plagiarism detection.
  • Video Script Timer: Estimate speaking duration from script word count. Adjust for speaking speed (slow, normal, fast). Perfect for YouTubers and presenters scripting to time limits.
  • YouTube Title Generator: Generate engaging YouTube titles with keyword optimization. Templates for tutorials, reviews, lists, and more.

Focus & Utility Tools

  • Metronome: Precise rhythm tool for musicians. Adjustable BPM (20-300), time signatures, and accent patterns. Also useful for runners maintaining cadence.
