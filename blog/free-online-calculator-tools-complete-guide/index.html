Health & Fitness Calculators

Health calculators help you understand your body and make informed decisions:

  • BMI Calculator: Body Mass Index using the WHO standard formula (weight/height²). Instant results with category interpretation. Remember: BMI doesn't account for muscle mass — athletes should also check the Body Fat Calculator.
  • Body Fat Calculator: Estimates body fat percentage using the U.S. Navy method (neck, waist, hip measurements). More nuanced than BMI alone.
  • Calorie Calculator: Daily calorie needs based on Mifflin-St Jeor equation. Accounts for age, weight, height, and activity level. Includes macro split suggestions.
  • Water Intake Calculator: Daily water needs based on weight and activity. Includes climate adjustment for hot environments.
  • Keto Calculator: Macronutrient targets for ketogenic diet. Calculates net carbs, protein, and fat targets based on your goals.
  • Pregnancy Due Date Calculator: Estimate due date from last menstrual period. Shows trimester milestones and weeks remaining.
