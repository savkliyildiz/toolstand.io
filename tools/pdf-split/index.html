    How to Use the PDF Split Tool

    1. Upload your PDF — Click the upload area or drag and drop your PDF file.
    2. Choose your split method — Enter custom page ranges (e.g., "1-3, 5, 7-10") or choose to extract every page as a separate PDF.
    3. Click Split — Press the "Split PDF" button and watch as your document is separated into individual files.
    4. Download your files — Each split PDF will appear with its own download button. You can download them individually or as a ZIP file.
