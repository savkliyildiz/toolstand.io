❓ Frequently Asked Questions

      

        What is the ideal title length for Google SERP?
        
Google typically displays the first 50-60 characters of a title tag. Titles longer than 60 characters may get truncated with an ellipsis (…). Aim for 50-60 characters to ensure your full title appears in search results without being cut off.

      

      

        Do meta tags help SEO?
        
Yes, meta tags influence click-through rates (CTR) from search results. While meta keywords are no longer a ranking factor, the title tag and meta description directly affect how your page appears in SERPs. A well-crafted title and description can significantly improve organic traffic even without changing your ranking position.

      

      

        What are Open Graph tags?
        
Open Graph (OG) tags are meta tags that control how your page appears when shared on social media platforms like Facebook, LinkedIn, Slack, and Discord. They define the title, description, image, and URL that appear in the shared preview card. Without OG tags, social platforms guess what to show, often resulting in poor previews.

      

      

        Can I preview mobile vs desktop SERP?
        
Google uses the same title and description for both mobile and desktop search results. However, on mobile devices, title tags may be truncated slightly earlier due to narrower screens. This tool shows the desktop SERP preview which covers the most common truncation points.
