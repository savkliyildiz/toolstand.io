How to Use the Water Intake Calculator

  1. Enter your weight — Type your body weight in kilograms (kg). The default is 75 kg.
  2. Select activity level — Choose Sedentary, Moderate (3-5x/week), Active (daily), or Athlete. Higher activity increases your water needs.
  3. Toggle hot climate — If you live in or are visiting a hot climate, check this box to add 500ml to your daily target.
  4. Read your result — Your recommended daily water intake appears instantly in liters and milliliters.
