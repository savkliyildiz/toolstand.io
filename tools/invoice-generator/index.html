How to Use the Invoice Generator

  1. Fill in your business details — add your company name, address, email, and phone in the "From" section. These appear at the top of the invoice.
  2. Enter client information — add the client's name, address, and email in the "Bill To" section.
  3. Add line items — describe each service or product, set quantity and unit price. Click "Add Line" for more items. Tax is calculated automatically based on your tax rate.
  4. Preview, print, or save as PDF — switch to the Preview tab to see the formatted invoice, then use your browser's Print function (Ctrl+P / Cmd+P) and choose "Save as PDF" to download.
