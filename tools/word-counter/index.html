Frequently Asked Questions

How does the word counter handle emojis, hashtags, and URLs?

URLs count as single words. Hashtags (#likeThis) count as one word. Emojis are excluded from word count but included in character count. Punctuation doesn't count as words.

What's the difference between character and word count for SEO?

For meta descriptions, Google displays ~155-160 characters — focus on character count. For blog posts, word count matters for content depth signals. Our tool shows both plus reading time.

How can word & character counter improve my daily workflow?

Eliminates the need to download apps or sign up for services. Bookmark it, use it in seconds, and get back to what matters. Embed it in your team dashboard.

Does word & character counter save my progress if I close the browser?
