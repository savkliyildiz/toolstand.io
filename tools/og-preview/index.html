How to Use the OG Preview Tool

  1. Enter your page details — fill in the title, description, URL, and image URL fields. These are the meta tags that social platforms read when someone shares your link.
  2. Watch the live previews — Facebook, Twitter, and LinkedIn card previews update in real time as you type. Character counters show remaining space for each platform's limits.
  3. Check platform-specific limits — Facebook truncates titles at ~60 chars and descriptions at ~110. Twitter cards have different dimensions. The previews show exactly how your link will appear.
  4. Copy the generated meta tags — once satisfied, copy the OG and Twitter Card meta tags to paste into your page's <head> section.
