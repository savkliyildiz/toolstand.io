How to Use the Pace Calculator

  1. Choose your calculation mode — select "Calculate Pace" (find pace from time + distance), "Calculate Time" (find finish time from pace + distance), or "Calculate Distance" (find distance from time + pace).
  2. Enter your known values — fill in the two fields that aren't grayed out. For time, use hours:minutes:seconds. For pace, use minutes:seconds per km or mile.
  3. Select distance and pace units — choose kilometers or miles. The pace unit can differ from the distance unit, e.g., pace per km for a race measured in miles.
  4. Check the race split table — scroll down to see estimated finish times for 5K, 10K, half marathon, and marathon at your current pace.
