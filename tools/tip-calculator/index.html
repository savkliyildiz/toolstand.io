How to Use the Tip Calculator

  1. Choose currency — Select USD ($), EUR (€), GBP (£), or TRY (₺) from the dropdown.
  2. Enter the bill — Type the total bill amount. Decimals are supported for precise amounts.
  3. Pick tip percentage — Click a preset (10%, 15%, 18%, 20%, 25%) or type a custom percentage. 15% is selected by default.
  4. Split the bill — Change the number of people to divide the total equally. The "Per Person" amount updates instantly. Toggle "Round up tip" for even numbers.
