How to Use the Keto & Macro Calculator

  1. Enter your body stats — fill in your gender, age, weight (kg), height (cm), and activity level. The calculator uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation to compute your BMR and TDEE.
  2. Choose your goal — select Maintain (weight stable), Fat Loss (500-calorie deficit with higher protein to preserve muscle), or Muscle Gain (calorie surplus with elevated protein).
  3. Set your carb limit — adjust the daily carb target (default 25g for strict keto, increase to 50g for a more flexible approach).
  4. Read your macro breakdown — review your daily fat, protein, and carb targets in grams. The doughnut chart and macro bar show the percentage split of your calories.
