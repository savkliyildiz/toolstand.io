Frequently Asked Questions

What's a healthy BMI range for adults?

WHO and CDC define healthy BMI as 18.5-24.9. Under 18.5 is underweight, 25-29.9 overweight, 30+ obese. BMI doesn't account for muscle mass — athletes often score 'overweight'.

Why does my BMI show overweight when I'm muscular?

BMI is a weight-to-height ratio that doesn't distinguish fat from muscle. Bodybuilders and athletes often get high scores. Use our Body Fat Calculator alongside BMI for a better picture.
