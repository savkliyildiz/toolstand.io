How to Use the FAQ Schema Generator

  1. Add your questions and answers. Type each question and its corresponding answer into the fields. The tool starts with two pre-filled pairs — edit them or remove them.
  2. Add more pairs as needed. Click + Add Question to include additional FAQ entries. You can remove any pair with the X button.
  3. Generate the schema. Click Generate Schema to produce valid JSON-LD structured data that follows the FAQPage schema.org specification.
  4. Copy and paste into your site. Click Copy to Clipboard and paste the JSON-LD into the <head> or <body> of your webpage.
