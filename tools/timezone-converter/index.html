How to Use the Time Zone Converter

  1. Set your reference time — Choose a date and time in the datetime picker (defaults to now).
  2. Add cities — Click "+ Add City" and pick from the preset list (Istanbul, New York, London, Tokyo, etc.). Add as many as you need.
  3. Compare times — Each city shows its local time side by side. Times automatically account for DST (daylight saving time).
  4. Plan meetings — Use the comparison to find overlapping business hours across time zones for scheduling calls and meetings.
