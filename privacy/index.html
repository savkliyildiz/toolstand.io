Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. No Personal Data Collection

ToolStand does not require accounts, logins, or any form of personal data submission. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other personally identifiable information. All tools work entirely in your browser with no server-side data storage.

2. Cookies & Third-Party Services

Google AdSense: We may display advertisements via Google AdSense. AdSense uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to ToolStand and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or by using the NAI opt-out tool.

3. Analytics

We may use privacy-focused analytics (such as Cloudflare Web Analytics or similar) that do not use cookies and do not track individual users across sites. These services provide aggregated, anonymized metrics like page view counts and referrer information. No personal data is collected or stored.

4. Local Storage

Some tools use your browser's local storage to remember preferences (such as your light/dark theme choice). This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

5. Children's Privacy

ToolStand does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. All tools are designed to function without any data collection, making them safe for all ages.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at hello@toolstand.io.
