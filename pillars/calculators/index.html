1. Finance & Money Calculators: Take Control of Your Financial Future

Financial literacy starts with understanding the numbers. ToolStand's finance calculators cover the four essential operations of personal finance: growing your money, borrowing money, investing systematically, and calculating returns. Every tool runs locally — your financial details never touch a server.

Compound Interest Calculator: The Eighth Wonder of the World

Albert Einstein allegedly called compound interest the eighth wonder of the world — and for good reason. The Compound Interest Calculator shows you exactly how a lump-sum investment grows over time with the power of compounding. Enter your initial investment, annual interest rate, compounding frequency (daily, monthly, quarterly, or annually), and time horizon, and the calculator produces a detailed growth projection complete with a Chart.js visualization that makes the exponential curve unmistakably clear.

Practical example: Invest $10,000 at a 7% annual return, compounded monthly for 30 years. The calculator shows you that your $10,000 becomes approximately $81,165 — and more importantly, the chart reveals how the growth accelerates dramatically in the later years. This is the tool you use when you're explaining to a younger family member why starting to invest at 25 instead of 35 can mean hundreds of thousands of dollars in difference by retirement.

The calculator also lets you toggle between nominal and inflation-adjusted returns, so you can see what your future money is actually worth in today's dollars. A 7% nominal return might look impressive, but after 3% annual inflation, the real purchasing power tells a more grounded story — and this calculator shows both.

Loan & Mortgage Calculator: Know Exactly What You're Signing Up For

Before you sign a mortgage or auto loan, the Loan & Mortgage Calculator gives you the full picture. Enter the loan amount, interest rate, and term, and it instantly calculates your monthly payment. But that's just the beginning — the real value is the full amortization schedule that breaks down every single payment into its principal and interest components.

For a $300,000 30-year mortgage at 6.5%, the calculator reveals a sobering truth: your first monthly payment of $1,896 allocates $1,625 to interest and only $271 to principal. By year 15, that ratio has flipped — and the amortization table shows exactly when. This transforms an abstract loan into a concrete, understandable financial commitment.

The early payoff calculator within the same tool is equally powerful. Add an extra $200 per month to that mortgage and the calculator shows you'll save over $90,000 in interest and pay off the loan nearly 7 years early. These are the numbers that motivate real financial behavior change — and they're all computed instantly, in your browser, with no loan officer watching over your shoulder.

DCA Calculator: Understand Your True Cost Basis

Dollar Cost Averaging (DCA) is one of the most popular investment strategies — invest a fixed dollar amount at regular intervals regardless of price. But tracking your true average cost across dozens or hundreds of purchases is tedious. The DCA Calculator solves this by letting you add multiple buy transactions — each with a price, quantity, and optional fee — and instantly computing your true weighted average cost per unit.

Practical use case: You've been buying Bitcoin weekly for 6 months at prices ranging from $62,000 to $98,000. You know you've spent $12,000 total, but what's your actual average purchase price? Plug each transaction into the DCA Calculator and you get the real number — plus the ability to model what would happen if you continued the strategy at current prices. This tool is essential for crypto and stock investors who want to know their genuine cost basis, not just a rough estimate.

Dividend Reinvestment Calculator: The Hidden Growth Engine

Dividend-paying stocks generate returns through two channels: price appreciation and dividend payouts. The Dividend Reinvestment Calculator (DRIP) models what happens when you reinvest those dividends back into more shares — and the results can be eye-opening. Enter the initial investment, share price, dividend yield, dividend growth rate, and time horizon. The calculator shows your position with and without reinvestment, revealing the dramatic difference that DRIP makes over decades.

A $25,000 investment in a stock with a 3.5% dividend yield and 5% annual dividend growth, reinvested over 25 years, can produce over $60,000 in additional value compared to taking dividends as cash — and all of that compounding happens on autopilot. The calculator accounts for tax if you choose to model taxable accounts, giving you the most realistic projection possible.

Tip & Bill Split Calculator: End the Awkward Dinner Math

Nobody wants to be the person fumbling with a calculator app while their friends wait. The Tip Calculator handles the two most common restaurant math problems: calculating the tip (with percentages that work worldwide — from the 15-20% standard in the US to the service-included customs elsewhere) and splitting the total bill evenly among any number of people. Enter the bill amount, select a tip percentage (or enter a custom one), choose the number of people splitting, and get the per-person total instantly. It even rounds up or down to the nearest dollar if you prefer clean numbers.

Inflation Calculator: What Will Your Money Be Worth?

The Inflation Calculator answers a deceptively simple question: what will $X be worth in Y years? Enter an amount, an inflation rate (the calculator provides historical averages as presets), and a time horizon. It shows you the future value in both nominal and real terms. If you're planning a retirement that's 20 years away, this tool helps you understand that $1 million in 2046 won't buy what $1 million buys today — at 3% annual inflation, it'll have the purchasing power of about $554,000 in today's dollars. This is the grounding tool every financial plan needs.

Savings & Retirement Calculators: The Long View

The Savings Calculator models recurring contributions with compound growth — ideal for projecting a 401(k), IRA, or general savings plan. Enter your current savings, monthly contribution, expected return, and time horizon for a Chart.js-powered growth visualization.

The Retirement Calculator takes this further, applying the 4% safe withdrawal rule to estimate how much monthly income your nest egg can sustain. It factors in expected retirement duration and produces a clear answer: save $X per month for Y years to have $Z per month in retirement. Both calculators help you move from vague financial anxiety to a concrete, actionable plan.

Trade Risk & Break-Even Calculator

Active traders face a specific math problem: at what price do you break even after commissions? The Trade Risk & Break-Even Calculator solves this with presets for major exchanges including BIST and Binance. Enter your buy price, quantity, and commission rate, and it calculates the exact break-even price you need to exit without a loss. This is the calculator you check before placing a trade — not after.

2. Health & Fitness Calculators: Understand Your Body With Numbers

Health metrics turn vague feelings into trackable data. ToolStand's health calculators cover body composition, nutrition, pregnancy, and fitness — all running locally so your medical data stays private.

BMI Calculator: The Universal Screening Tool

The BMI Calculator is the most widely used body composition screening tool in the world — and for good reason. Enter your height and weight (in metric or imperial units), and it instantly calculates your Body Mass Index along with your weight category: underweight, normal weight, overweight, or obese. The calculator uses the standard formula — weight (kg) / height (m)² — and color-codes the result for immediate readability.

Important context the calculator provides: BMI is a population-level screening tool, not a diagnostic instrument. It doesn't distinguish between muscle and fat, so a muscular athlete might register as "overweight" despite being extremely lean. For a more complete picture, pair the BMI Calculator with the Body Fat Calculator, which uses the US Navy method (neck, waist, and hip circumference measurements) to estimate body fat percentage. Together, these two calculators give you a much more accurate understanding of your body composition than either alone.

Body Fat Calculator: Beyond BMI

The Body Fat Calculator uses the US Navy circumference method — one of the most validated body fat estimation formulas available outside of a lab. For men, it uses neck and waist measurements; for women, neck, waist, and hip measurements. The result is a body fat percentage estimate with gender-specific fitness categories (essential fat, athletic, fit, acceptable, or obese). This calculator is particularly valuable for people engaged in strength training who find that BMI doesn't accurately reflect their body composition.

Keto & Macro Calculator: Your Personalized Nutrition Blueprint

Starting a ketogenic diet without understanding your macros is like navigating without a map. The Keto & Macro Calculator begins by estimating your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation — the most accurate BMR formula for the general population. It then multiplies your BMR by your activity level to produce your Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) — the calories you actually burn each day.

From there, it applies ketogenic diet ratios to break down your daily targets into grams of protein, fat, and carbohydrates, adjusted for your goal (weight loss, maintenance, or muscle gain). The results display in a color-coded doughnut chart that makes the macro distribution instantly intuitive. Practical example: a 35-year-old female, 165 cm, 70 kg, moderately active, targeting weight loss, might get a recommendation of 1,650 calories with 20g carbs, 95g protein, and 130g fat — all calculated in under a second, entirely in her browser.

Calorie & TDEE Calculator: Fuel for Your Goals

For those not following a specific diet but wanting to understand their calorie needs, the Calorie & TDEE Calculator provides the foundational numbers. It calculates your BMR, applies an activity multiplier for TDEE, and then suggests calorie targets for weight loss, maintenance, and muscle gain. It also provides a macronutrient breakdown based on standard dietary guidelines. Whether you're cutting, bulking, or maintaining, this calculator gives you the numbers you need to eat with intention.

Pregnancy Due Date Calculator: Your Countdown Starts Here

The Pregnancy Due Date Calculator uses Naegele's Rule — the standard obstetric method — to estimate a due date from the first day of your last menstrual period (LMP). Enter the LMP date, and the calculator adds 280 days (40 weeks) to produce the estimated due date, along with the conception window and current trimester. It also shows how far along you are in weeks and days. This is the same calculation your OB-GYN performs at your first prenatal visit — made available instantly, without any health data being transmitted or stored.

Water Intake Calculator: Hydration, Personalized

How much water should you actually drink? The Water Intake Calculator goes beyond the "8 glasses a day" rule. It factors in your weight, activity level, and climate to produce a personalized daily water recommendation. An active 80 kg person in a hot climate has very different hydration needs than a sedentary 55 kg person in a cool office — and this calculator accounts for those differences.

Pace & Running Calculator: Your Race, Your Numbers

Runners live by their splits. The Pace & Running Calculator handles all the essential running math: given any two of pace, time, and distance, it calculates the third. It also provides split times for standard race distances — 5K, 10K, half marathon, and marathon — so you know exactly what pace you need to hit your target finish time. Training for a sub-4-hour marathon? The calculator tells you that requires a 5:41 per kilometer pace, and shows your projected splits at every marker.

5. How It Works: Privacy & Client-Side Computation

Every calculator on ToolStand runs entirely in your browser. When you enter your weight and height into the BMI Calculator, the formula executes as JavaScript in your browser tab. When you model compound interest projections, the iterative calculation runs locally. When you enter financial details into the loan calculator, that data never crosses the network.

This architectural choice isn't just a technical detail — it's a privacy commitment with specific implications:

  • Your financial data stays private. Loan amounts, investment values, retirement projections, income figures — all computed locally. No server ever sees your financial details.
  • Your health data is yours alone. Height, weight, body measurements, pregnancy dates, dietary macros — all processed in your browser and discarded when you close the tab. No health data is ever stored, logged, or transmitted.
  • Nothing is saved. Close the tab and every number you've entered is gone. There's no user account, no calculation history on a server, no data retention of any kind.
  • Works offline after loading. Once the page is cached, most calculators continue working without an internet connection. The math is just JavaScript — it runs wherever JavaScript runs.

The technologies powering these calculators include standard JavaScript for arithmetic and formula evaluation, the Chart.js library for visualizations (compound interest growth curves, macro doughnut charts, amortization graphs), and browser-native APIs for rendering. The Scientific Calculator uses the standard JavaScript Math object for trigonometric and logarithmic functions — the same precision you'd get from any JavaScript runtime.

Compare this to calculator apps that require installation, request network permissions, or upload your data to cloud servers for "syncing." Those apps create privacy risks that ToolStand's client-side architecture eliminates entirely. Your browser is a powerful computation engine — and these calculators prove it.

The Compound Interest Calculator models growth of a single lump-sum investment with compound interest — good for understanding how a one-time investment grows. The Savings Calculator models recurring contributions (like monthly deposits) with compound growth — better for projecting retirement accounts or regular savings plans. Use the first for inheritance or windfall planning, the second for paycheck-to-paycheck savings strategies.

Yes. The Loan & Mortgage Calculator includes a full amortization table showing the principal and interest breakdown for each payment period. You can see the interest-to-principal ratio shift over time and understand the total cost of the loan. It uses Naegele's Rule, the standard obstetric method: first day of last menstrual period plus 280 days (40 weeks). This produces an estimated due date, conception window, and current trimester — the same calculation your healthcare provider performs at your first prenatal visit. What does the Keto Macro Calculator actually calculate?

It first estimates your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, then calculates your Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) based on activity level. Does the DCA Calculator handle transaction fees?
