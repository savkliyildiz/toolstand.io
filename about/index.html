About ToolStand

Free tools should be simple, fast, and available to everyone.

Our Mission

ToolStand was built on a simple belief: the tools you need every day should not require downloads, subscriptions, or personal data. A spirit level should just work when you need to hang a picture. A QR code should generate instantly without a $10/week app store subscription.

We are tired of simple utilities wrapped in paywalls, ads that break functionality, and tools that demand your email before they do anything useful. ToolStand is our answer: clean, free tools that work in any browser, on any device, with zero friction.

The Free Tools Philosophy

We believe free means genuinely free — not "free trial," not "freemium," not "free if you watch three ads." Every tool on ToolStand is fully functional at no cost. No account required. No premium tier. No catch.

We keep the lights on through unobtrusive display advertising (via AdSense) that does not interfere with tool functionality. No pop-ups, no forced video ads, no dark patterns. If you prefer, you can support us by telling a friend about ToolStand — that means more to us than any subscription fee.
